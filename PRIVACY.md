# Tic Tac Troll privacy policy

*Last updated: September 27, 2026*

Tic Tac Troll does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- The app has no internet access, so nothing you do in it leaves your device.
- It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting, or accounts.
- It keeps two settings on your device, whether sound is on and whether Bored trolls mode is on, so they're remembered between games. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Questions? Contact the developer at the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
